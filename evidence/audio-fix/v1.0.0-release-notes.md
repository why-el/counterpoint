The first release of Counterpoint: a marble music box with eight pitches, a wheel you can rewind, and arrangements you can share.

[Play Counterpoint](https://why-el.github.io/counterpoint/). Press Sound to listen.

The attached archive preserves version 1.0.0, built from `c7c18ac`. It passed 12 core tests, browser controls in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, and two checks of the published build. [Test results](https://github.com/why-el/counterpoint/blob/master/REVIEW_LOG.md) record the evidence and coverage limits.
